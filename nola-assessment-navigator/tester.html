Help test the NOLA Assessment Navigator

Thank you for helping. This takes about 15 minutes. The Navigator is a tool that explains New Orleans property assessments. It is not an official tool. I'm testing the tool, not you: if something is confusing, that's what I need to know.

Privacy: don't type your name, Social Security number or income anywhere. You can use your own address or this sample one: 1205 St. Charles Ave, Apt 607. Nothing on this page is sent anywhere by itself: at the end you copy your answers and send them to Ethan.

Step 1. Open the Navigator in a new tab

Open the NOLA Assessment Navigator and keep this page open.

Step 2. Try these five things

  1. Ask it a question you really have about property taxes or assessments, in your own words.
  2. Find a property (yours or the sample) and confirm it.
  3. Ask: How much tax will I pay?
  4. Ask anything else you're curious about for that property (your neighborhood, home prices, exemptions).
  5. Ask: Should I appeal my assessment? Then press Prepare a note to hand this to a person.
