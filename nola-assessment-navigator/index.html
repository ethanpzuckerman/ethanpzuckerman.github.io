Data & sources

Where every number comes from

State tax rolls

Louisiana Tax Commission files for Orleans Parish: the 2026 roll and the 2027 roll filed Sept. 4, 2026, including every levy and computed tax per parcel. Values are as of Jan. 1, 2023.

Official sources

The Louisiana Constitution, the City's 2026 millage table, the Legislative Auditor's review of the 2024 reappraisal, and the City Council, read directly and logged by date.

Census and Zillow

American Community Survey 2020–2024 by block group, matched to 86% of parcels, and Zillow's home value index by ZIP code through Sept. 2026.

Privacy

Owner names, mailing addresses and sale prices are never shown. Census figures describe neighborhoods, never owners. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

    Limits

    What it can't do

    • No advice. It explains how the system works. Whether to appeal, or what your home is worth, is for you and the Assessor.
    • The 2027 bill isn't final. The state file predates the City Council's Nov. 5, 2026 roll-forward, so the increase is shown as a range.
    • Not in the roll: square footage, bedrooms, condition, and special-district fees such as the Downtown Development District.
    • Some answers need confirming. When a source couldn't be re-checked, the answer says so and points you to the right office.
